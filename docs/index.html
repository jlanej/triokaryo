Large chromosomal events in a trio,
read from its VCF alone.

Depth, B-allele bands and transmission phasing from one trio VCF: every gain, loss, copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity and uniparental disomy, with its share of cells, its parent of origin, whether the parent's two copies are one homologue or two, and its edges at site resolution. No alignments, no array, no joint cohort call.

Two public trios

Illumina's DRAGEN 3.7.6 re-analysis of the 1000 Genomes high-coverage genomes (AWS Open Data), three per-sample VCFs merged into one trio VCF each. Nothing here was tuned to them; the events were known from NGS-DOSE's alignment-free karyotype and checked against DRAGEN's own CNV caller afterwards.

NA12739, a CEU trio (father NA12748, mother NA12749)

Genome-wide: LRR with its step fit and the B-allele fraction with the phased fraction over it, per member; the child over the parents' mean; the child's maternal and paternal copies.
  • Trisomy 12 in the child, 96% of cells by depth, 95% by the phased bands. The extra copy is paternal (17,630 phased sites), and the two paternal copies are one homologue throughout: a mitotic duplication or a meiosis II error, not a meiosis I nondisjunction. Neither parent carries it. NGS-DOSE: 47,XY,+12; DRAGEN: CN 3 along the chromosome.
  • A gain of 13q in the father from 88 Mb to the end in 69% of his cells, on the homologue he passed to the child (who did not inherit the gain). DRAGEN: CN 3. NGS-DOSE read 1.999 copies of chromosome 13 and missed it.
  • A loss of the end of 21q in the father, 42.8 to 46.1 Mb, 59% of cells, found by the phased bands alone: four bins long, under the depth scan's five-bin floor. DRAGEN: CN 1 over 44.2 to 46.7 Mb.
  • A 12% gain of the first 5 Mb of chromosome 1 by depth, which the phased bands put at 4%: flagged as a likely GC artefact.

HG01103, a Puerto Rican trio (father HG01101, mother HG01102)

The same eight rows. The child's chromosome 2 and 14 losses stand out in the depth, the phased fraction and the copies; the father's homozygous stretches are runs of homozygosity, not disomies.
  • A loss of 2q from 141 to 173 Mb in 67% of the child's cells (57% by the phased bands), then 173 to 180 Mb in 31%: the paternal copy is the one lost (4,060 phased sites). The edge at 173.16 Mb to site resolution. NGS-DOSE: −2(140–179)[0.68]; DRAGEN: CN 1 in four pieces.

Reading the figures

Each chromosome figure has five rows per member; the genome figure has the first two per member plus the within-family depth and the child's parental copies. The raw data stay in every figure: the smoothed tracks sit beside them, never instead of them. The full guide, with pattern cards of what each kind of event looks like and every column explained, is written beside every page: guide.html.

1 · LRRlog2 of each 1-Mb bin's median depth over the member's autosomal median, GC- and panel-corrected, with its step fit (black) and the calls as coloured lines. A gain of one copy in a share f of cells reads log2(1 + f/2).
2 · BAFthe raw alt-allele fraction at the member's heterozygous sites. In the child the sites where the parents are opposite homozygotes are coloured by the parent of the alt allele: under a gain the duplicated parent's allele sits at 2/3, under a loss the retained parent's at 1.
3 · phased fractionthe maternal allele's fraction along the child, the transmitted allele's along a parent: every phased site (faint), pooled windows (green, hollow where parted in everyone) and the step fit. One half where the homologues are equal; 1/2 ± d along an event, and the band is the parent: in the child, below one half the paternal homologue is in excess (a paternal gain, a maternal copy lost), above one half the maternal; in a parent, below one half the event lies on the homologue not passed to the child, above one half on the one passed. Thin lines: the auxiliary tracks that leave the main one where the child carries two different homologues of one parent.
4 · copiesthe LRR step fit's copies split by the fraction's: maternal and paternal along the child, passed and not passed along a parent. A trisomy's extra copy, a deletion's missing one, a disomy's two from one parent, each with its parent named.
5 · het rateheterozygous calls per confident call, per bin: zero under a loss of heterozygosity in every cell.

The method

One pass over the PASS biallelic SNVs of a trio VCF (GATK joint calls, or per-sample calls merged with bcftools merge -0). Three signals, one reading.

  1. Depth → LRRper 1-Mb bin, the median depth over the member's autosomal median; a reference panel of other genomes called the same way takes out the reference's own depth structure (centromere flanks, segmental duplications); a running median against GC takes out the rest. Segmented recursively; a gain or loss needs 10% of cells and five bins.
  2. Bands → copy-neutral LOHthe deviation d of the heterozygous sites' alt fraction from one half, by a depth-aware maximum likelihood (not |BAF − ½|, which inflates at low depth), and the heterozygosity rate; a gain reads d = f/(2(2+f)), a loss f/(2(2−f)), an LOH f/2: the share of cells a second time, independently of the depth.
  3. Transmission phasingwherever the parents are opposite homozygotes the child's alleles have known parents; wherever the child is homozygous a parent's transmitted allele is known. The signed, pooled fraction is read without the folding bias, fitted by exact total-variation denoising, and scanned for what the depth cannot see: a gain or loss in a few per cent of cells, a uniparental heterodisomy.
  4. One homologue or twothe sites where only one parent is homozygous phase the child too, but read the wrong way wherever the child carries two different homologues of one parent. Drawn as auxiliary tracks, they part from the main one along a meiotic trisomy or heterodisomy and return at each crossover: meiotic against mitotic, with the crossovers mapped.
  5. The trio readinginheritance (the same event in a parent), the parent of origin two ways (a likelihood ratio over the opposite-homozygote sites, and the phased sign), the Mendelian-error rate (a run of homozygosity has none, a uniparental isodisomy has them at every informative site), the edges at site resolution.
  6. The checksa window parted in two or more members is paralogy, not an event; a phased find needs 2 Mb, a consistent run of windows and a 6% share; a depth call whose phased share is under half of it is flagged as a likely artefact; given events (NGS-DOSE, DRAGEN, a clinical karyotype) are matched and the concordance tabulated.
The method and every threshold are in METHODS.md; every column of every table in OUTPUT.md. A mock trio with planted events (a maternal meiotic trisomy, a paternal mitotic mosaic, a heterodisomy, an isodisomy, an inherited deletion, an 8% gain under the depth's threshold) is the test bed: triokaryo mock.

Run it

Python 3.9 or later with pysam, numpy and matplotlib; or the container.

pip install git+https://github.com/jlanej/triokaryo
triokaryo run --vcf trio.vcf.gz --pedigree trios.tsv --child KID --out out/KID \
    --panel 1kg-dragen --events other_calls.tsv --gc-track gc.tsv
triokaryo cohort --runs 'out/*' --out cohort --events other_calls.tsv

--panel 1kg-dragen is the shipped panel of twelve public 1000 Genomes genomes called by DRAGEN; for another pipeline build one from that cohort (triokaryo panel --vcfs ..., or from a first pass's bins.tsv files with --runs). Without a panel the reference's own depth structure reads as events. The two trios above reproduce with docs/example/fetch_1kg_trio.sh.
