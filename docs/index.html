Large chromosomal events in a trio,
from its VCF.

Depth, B-allele bands and transmission phasing from one trio VCF: every gain, loss, copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity and uniparental disomy, with its cell fraction, its parent of origin, whether the two copies from that parent are one homologue or two, and its boundaries at site resolution. No alignments, no array, no cohort-wide joint call.

Two public trios

Illumina's DRAGEN 3.7.6 re-analysis of the 1000 Genomes high-coverage genomes (AWS Open Data): three per-sample VCFs merged into one trio VCF each, analysed with the shipped reference panel and no GC track. No parameter was tuned to these trios. The events were known from NGS-DOSE's alignment-free karyotype and compared with DRAGEN's CNV calls afterwards; note that the six members of these two trios are among the twelve genomes of the shipped panel (see the example README).

NA12739, a CEU trio (father NA12748, mother NA12749)

Genome-wide: per member the LRR with its step fit and the BAF with the pooled phased fraction; the child's depth over the parents' mean; the child's maternal and paternal copy number.
  • Trisomy 12 in the child: cell fraction 0.96 by depth, 0.95 by the phased track. The extra copy is paternal (17,630 phased sites) and the two paternal copies are one homologue throughout: a mitotic duplication or a meiosis II error, not a meiosis I nondisjunction. Neither parent carries it. NGS-DOSE: 47,XY,+12; DRAGEN: CN 3 along the chromosome.
  • Gain of 13q in the father, 88 Mb to the telomere, in 69% of his cells, on the homologue he transmitted; the child did not inherit the gain. DRAGEN: CN 3. NGS-DOSE reported 1.999 copies of chromosome 13.
  • Loss of terminal 21q in the father, 42.8 Mb to the telomere, in 59% of cells, detected by the phased scan alone: four bins, under the depth segmentation's five-bin floor. DRAGEN: CN 1 over 44.2–46.7 Mb.
  • A 12% gain of chr1:0–5 Mb by depth that the phased track puts at 4%: flagged as a possible depth artefact.

HG01103, a Puerto Rican trio (father HG01101, mother HG01102)

The same eight rows. The child's losses on chromosomes 2 and 14 are visible in the depth, the phased fraction and the copy number; the father's homozygous segments are runs of homozygosity, not disomies.

Reading the figures

A chromosome figure has five rows per member; the genome figure has the first two per member plus the within-family depth track and the child's parental copy number. The raw data remain in every figure; smoothed tracks are drawn beside them, never instead of them. The guide, with pattern cards of each event type and every column defined, is written beside every page: guide.html.

1 · LRRlog2 of each 1-Mb bin's median depth over the member's autosomal median, GC- and panel-corrected where applicable, with its step fit (black) and the calls as coloured lines. A one-copy gain in a cell fraction f reads log2(1 + f/2).
2 · BAFthe alt-allele read fraction at the member's heterozygous sites. In the child, the informative sites (parents opposite homozygotes) are coloured by the parent of the alt allele: under a gain the duplicated parent's allele lies at 2/3, under a loss the retained parent's at 1.
3 · phased fractionthe maternal-allele fraction along the child, the transmitted-allele fraction along a parent: each phased site (faint), pooled windows (green; hollow where shared by two or more members) and the step fit. 1/2 with equal homologue copy; 1/2 ± d along an event, with the sign giving the parent: in the child, below 1/2 the paternal homologue is in excess (paternal gain or maternal loss) and above 1/2 the maternal; in a parent, below 1/2 the event lies on the untransmitted homologue and above 1/2 on the transmitted one. Thin lines: the auxiliary tracks, which depart from the main track where the child carries two different homologues of one parent.
4 · copiesthe LRR step fit's copy number split by the fraction's step fit: maternal and paternal along the child, transmitted and untransmitted along a parent. A trisomy's extra copy, a deletion's missing copy and a disomy's two copies from one parent each appear with the parent named.
5 · het rateheterozygous calls per confident call, per bin: zero under a constitutional loss of heterozygosity.

The method

One pass over the PASS biallelic SNVs of a trio VCF (a joint-called family VCF, or per-sample VCFs merged with bcftools merge -0). Three signals, one analysis.

  1. Depth → LRRper 1-Mb bin, the median depth over the member's autosomal median. A reference panel of other genomes called the same way removes the reference's own depth structure (centromere flanks, segmental duplications); a running median against GC removes GC bias when a GC track is given. Binary segmentation; a gain or loss requires five bins and a cell fraction of about 10%.
  2. Bands → copy-neutral LOHthe deviation d of the heterozygous sites' alt fraction from 1/2 by a depth-aware maximum likelihood (not the mean of |BAF − ½|, which inflates at low depth), and the heterozygosity rate. A gain gives d = f/(2(2+f)), a loss f/(2(2−f)), CN-LOH f/2: a second estimate of the cell fraction, independent of the depth.
  3. Transmission phasingwhere the parents are opposite homozygotes the child's alleles have known parental origin; where the child is homozygous a parent's transmitted allele is known. The signed, pooled fraction is free of the folding bias, fitted by exact total-variation denoising, and scanned for events below the depth detection limit: a gain or loss in a few per cent of cells, a uniparental heterodisomy.
  4. One homologue or two, and the meiotic stagesites where only one parent is homozygous also phase the child but are misassigned wherever the child carries two different homologues of one parent. Drawn as auxiliary tracks, they depart from the main track along a meiotic trisomy or heterodisomy and rejoin at each crossover. The state at the centromere classifies a whole-chromosome event as meiosis I (heterodisomic), meiosis II (isodisomic, with heterodisomic segments beyond a crossover) or mitotic (isodisomic throughout), and each change of state places a crossover.
  5. Trio analysisinheritance (an event of the same type in a parent, reciprocal overlap of 50%), the parent of origin two ways (a likelihood ratio over the informative sites, and the sign of the phased shift), the Mendelian-error rate (none in a run of homozygosity; errors at every informative site in a uniparental isodisomy), and boundaries at site resolution.
  6. Sex chromosomesX and Y copy number per member, the complement (XY, XX, XXY, X, XYY) checked against the pedigree sex, and whole-X or whole-Y events against the expected complement: 47,XXY with its parent of origin and meiotic stage, 45,X and its mosaics, mosaic loss of Y from the panel or from the father/son Y depth ratio.
  7. Checksa window shifted in two or more members is paralogy, not an event; a phased-scan event requires 2 Mb, a consistent run of windows and a shift of 0.015; a depth call whose phased cell fraction is under half of the depth's is flagged as a possible artefact; supplied events (NGS-DOSE, DRAGEN, a clinical karyotype) are matched and the concordance tabulated.
The method and every threshold are in METHODS.md; every column of every table in OUTPUT.md. A simulated trio with planted events (a maternal meiotic trisomy, a paternal mitotic mosaic, a heterodisomy, an isodisomy, an inherited deletion, an 8% gain below the depth threshold) is the test bed: triokaryo mock.

Run it

Python 3.9 or later with pysam, numpy and matplotlib; or the container.

pip install git+https://github.com/jlanej/triokaryo
triokaryo run --vcf trio.vcf.gz --pedigree trios.tsv --child KID --out out/KID \
    --panel 1kg-dragen --events other_calls.tsv --gc-track gc.tsv
triokaryo cohort --runs 'out/*' --out cohort --events other_calls.tsv

--panel 1kg-dragen is the shipped panel of twelve public 1000 Genomes genomes called by DRAGEN; for another pipeline build one from that cohort (triokaryo panel --vcfs ..., or from a first pass's bins.tsv files with --runs). Without a panel the reference's own depth structure is called as events. The two trios above are reproduced by docs/example/fetch_1kg_trio.sh.
