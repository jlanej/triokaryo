# Calibration grid

Generated by

```bash
triokaryo calibrate --out docs/calibration --cell-fractions 0.05,0.1,0.2,0.3,0.5,1 --depths 15,30,60 --sizes 5,10,20,50 \
    --whole --replicates 2 --sites-per-mb 600 \
    --contigs chr1,chr2,chr3,chr4,chr5,chr6,chr7,chr8,chr9,chr10,chr11,chr12,chr13,chr14,chr15,chr16,chr17,chr18 --seed 1
```

Thirty-six simulated trios (six cell fractions × three depths × two replicates) at 600 PASS SNVs per Mb (real WGS carries
about 1,000), each carrying a gain, a loss and a copy-neutral LOH of 5, 10, 20 and 50 Mb on twelve chromosomes (the
duplicated, lost or replaced copy paternal), a whole-chromosome maternal meiosis I trisomy (chr17) and a maternal
heterodisomy (chr18), with four unaffected chromosomes. `calibration.md` holds the detection tables with Wilson 95%
intervals, the whole-chromosome results, the calls on unaffected chromosomes and the cell-fraction errors;
`calibration.tsv` one row per planted event; `false_positives.tsv` the calls on unaffected chromosomes;
`calibration.png` the detection grid per depth with the source of each call (D depth, B bands, P phased). Two replicates
give wide intervals (0–66% for 0 of 2, 34–100% for 2 of 2); the pattern across neighbouring cells is the reading.

Reading, at this density:

- **Floors by depth.** Segmental events of 5 Mb need a cell fraction of about 0.5 at 15×, 0.3 at 30× (0.2 for losses)
  and 0.2 at 60×; events of 10 Mb and up are found from 0.2 at every depth, and from 0.1 at 60× (partly at 30×); nothing
  is found at 0.05 at any depth. The depth calls most segmental events; the phased scan adds those near the depth floor.
- **Whole chromosomes.** The maternal trisomy and the heterodisomy are found from a cell fraction of 0.1 at every depth
  (half the replicates at 0.05 and 30×), with the parent of origin always correct. A heterodisomy below f = 1 is read as
  mosaic copy-neutral LOH, which the method cannot tell from a mosaic heterodisomy; at f = 1 it is UPD. The meiotic stage is
  assigned from a cell fraction of 0.2 and is then always meiosis I; below a phased shift of 0.03 (a cell fraction of about
  0.13) the homologue test is not attempted, so a 10% mosaic carries no stage rather than a wrong one.
- **No false call** on an unaffected chromosome in any of the 36 runs.
- **Cell fraction.** The depth estimate is within 0.004 at every depth; the folded-band estimate within 0.03–0.06 at 15×
  and 0.01–0.02 at 30×; the phased estimate within 0.02 at 15× and 0.01 at 30× and 60×.

The first run of this grid, with the bin depth as the median over the bin's sites, found none of the 10- and 20-Mb
gains and losses even at f = 1: the median of hundreds of integer depths is quantised to one read, the robust noise
scale of the LRR track was exactly zero, and the depth segmentation skipped those chromosomes while the phased scan
typed the events as LOH against a "flat" depth. The bin depth is now a 20% trimmed mean and the noise scale has a
counting-noise floor; the grid is the regression check for such changes.
