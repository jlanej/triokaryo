# Calibration grid

Generated by

```bash
triokaryo calibrate --out docs/calibration --cell-fractions 0.05,0.1,0.2,0.3,0.5,1 --depths 30 --sizes 5,10,20,50 \
    --sites-per-mb 600 --contigs chr1,chr2,chr3,chr4,chr5,chr6,chr7,chr8,chr9,chr10,chr11,chr12,chr13,chr14,chr15,chr16 --seed 1
```

One simulated trio per cell fraction at 30× with 600 PASS SNVs per Mb (real WGS carries about 1,000), carrying a gain, a
loss and a copy-neutral LOH of 5, 10, 20 and 50 Mb on twelve chromosomes (the duplicated, lost or replaced copy
paternal), with four unaffected chromosomes. `calibration.md` holds the detection tables and the cell-fraction errors,
`calibration.tsv` one row per planted event, `calibration.png` the detection grid with the source of each call (D depth,
B bands, P phased). One replicate per cell; rates are therefore 0% or 100%, and a single miss (the 10% losses of 20 and
50 Mb, while the 10-Mb loss was found) reflects a call near its threshold rather than a size dependence.

Reading, at this depth and density:

- Constitutional events (f = 1) and mosaics down to a cell fraction of 0.3 are found at every size from 5 Mb, with the
  cell fraction within 0.02 and the parent of origin correct.
- At f = 0.2, events of 10 Mb and up are found (losses from 5 Mb); at f = 0.1, 20 Mb and up; nothing at f = 0.05, which
  is below the floors of both the depth (about 10%) and the phased scan (about 6% for whole chromosomes).
- Copy-neutral LOH is found by the bands (constitutional) or the phased scan (mosaic), gains and losses by the depth
  and, near its floor, by the phased scan typed by the depth's direction.
- No call was made on an unaffected chromosome in any run.

The first run of this grid, with the bin depth as the median over the bin's sites, found none of the 10- and 20-Mb
gains and losses even at f = 1: the median of hundreds of integer depths is quantised to one read, the robust noise
scale of the LRR track was exactly zero, and the depth segmentation skipped those chromosomes while the phased scan
typed the events as LOH against a "flat" depth. The bin depth is now a 20% trimmed mean and the noise scale has a
counting-noise floor; the grid is the regression check for such changes.
